Three main contributions: explicit continuation targets; occurrence/type-aware
empirical identification with a new prospective contrast; component-specific
failure explanations connected to conditional progress assumptions. Native
coverage, output laws, ID controls and provenance support these contributions.
No new theorem of general algorithm learning, uniform CF superiority or
universal distributed correctness is claimed. Independent scientific review
remains pending; functional checks are not that review.

The gate, class/support audit, coarser logical grouping and selected BE diagnoses are post-test
descriptive additions. They do not replace the original endpoint, split,
models or outcomes. The complete intervention/key is specified in the paper's
prospective appendix and `scripts/audit-revision-continuations.py` produces
the class/support evidence from frozen banks. Separately,
`scripts/probe-revision-be-failures.py` reproduces frozen-model fit/test
queries and the original-cohort participation witness, with input hashes in
`results/paper-revision-20261030/be-failure-diagnostics.json`.
The original source freeze seals
its enumerated files; it is not a full dependency/environment lock.
