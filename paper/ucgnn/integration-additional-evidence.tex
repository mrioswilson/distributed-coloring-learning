\subsection{Label budgets and neighbor-context coverage}\label{sec:additional}
A separate E1 study uses five paired seeds per teacher (95101--95105),
4,000 fixed updates and passive, matched and CF procedures at low and high
acquired-label budgets for BE, GM and ColorTrials: 100 trainings, with
1,500-update snapshots as a secondary milestone. It uses fresh training,
continuation and functional namespaces. The high native controls acquire
additional native trajectories; low-budget CF removes stored continuation
labels to keep its total fixed. The BE high CF/native totals are
6,776/6,774, GM high totals differ by at most two labels per seed, and
ColorTrials uses 70,000/69,999. These small component-group rounding
differences are recorded, rather than called exact budget equality.
They remain full acquisition-procedure contrasts: the native controls
receive no alternative source of informative occupied-option negatives.

CF improves standard-family BA in each BE/GM pair, including low-budget CF
against high-budget native supervision. Yet the BE high-CF functional loss
replicates the separation between continuation metrics and completion.
Across all eight BE arms only 32/40 checkpoints pass all 1,000 functional
runs; all adverse arms remain recorded. The new study's own criterion of
at least 99\% BA on unseen sufficient types fails: only two of five high-CF
seeds reach it. BE's eligibility/participation diagnostic scores 99.26\%
occurrence BA. A holdout of occupied queries at late KW bounds tests a
generator-level combination; it does not by itself establish new effective
inputs, because queried BE COMMIT does not read the numerical bound.
Received-neighbor message perturbations, unused in these training banks,
also expose errors. Thus the stronger standard-family advantage does not
contradict the input-context holdout result. Its 1,500-update CF snapshots
already have high standard BA, so update count alone does not explain the
cross-study difference.

\paragraph{A targeted follow-up with a new battery.}
After inspecting those results, a separately frozen follow-up perturbs
neighbor messages in the high-CF BE bank, preserving roots, root queries
and all labels. BE's oracle ignores those neighbor participation/offering
messages when occupancy is fixed. Five new paired trainings use a fresh
continuation and 1,000-run functional battery, together with the frozen
controls. The perturbed arm accepts all positive active-free-neighbor queries
in all five seeds and completes 5,000/5,000 runs. The original high-CF
seed 95105 again rejects those positives and completes 301/1,000, while its
perturbed counterpart completes 1,000/1,000. This is a positive result for
that context intervention on the new battery, not a universal CF remedy.
It addresses a different error from the $\kappa=0$, silent-neighbor
false negatives in seeds 105001/105008.
